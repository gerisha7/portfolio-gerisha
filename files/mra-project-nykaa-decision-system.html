Root Cause Analysis

🔍 Finding 1 — Marketing Cut: Local marketing spend (INV-3.4) was cut by 68% between W2 (₹84K) and W4 (₹30K). Conversion dropped from 21% → 14% in the same window.

🔍 Finding 2 — Inventory Depletion: Stock levels dropped from 94% to 75% over 8 weeks. Top 50 SKUs had 14% stock-out rate in W6-W8.

🔍 Finding 3 — Budget Reallocation: The ₹54K/week marketing cut was redirected to high-performing Bandra (NYK-BOM-014) and Lower Parel stores — inadvertently starving Powai.

Recommended Action: 1) Restore local marketing to ₹75-85K/week for 4 weeks. 2) Replenish top 50 SKUs to 95%+ stock levels. 3) If conversion doesn't recover to 20%+ within 4 weeks, consider store relocation. The ₹30K/week "saving" was destroying ₹8-10L/week in store revenue.

07 · Business Selection, Decisions & Decision Makers

Why this page exists: Before designing any system, we must define the business context, the decisions to support, and who makes those decisions.

Nykaa — Business Model

Nykaa (FSN E-Commerce Ventures Ltd) is India's leading beauty and personal care omnichannel retailer. Business Units:

Marketplace (54% GMV)
3,000+ brands, 2.5L+ SKUs listed. Commission-based model. Lowest margin but highest reach.
Owned Brands (33% GMV)
Nykaa Cosmetics, Kay Beauty, Wanderlust, Nykaa Naturals. Highest margin (27.3%).
Offline Retail (14% GMV)
180+ stores across 70+ cities. Experience-driven. Lower digital efficiency.

Target Customers: Urban women 18-45, premium beauty enthusiasts, Tier 1-2 expansion. Why this system is needed: Nykaa spends ₹500+ Cr annually on marketing and operations across 3 SBUs — without a unified decision system, money leaks across silos.

Key Business Decisions

📊 Campaign Optimization: Which campaigns to scale, pause, or kill?

🏪 Store Performance: Which stores need intervention vs expansion?

💰 Category Investment: Which categories deserve more inventory & marketing?

📈 Growth Strategy: Where will the next ₹100 Cr of GMV come from?

⚖️ ROI Trade-offs: Short-term revenue vs long-term brand building?

16 · Assumptions

Why this page exists: Every design choice that can't be verified with real data must be documented as an assumption so evaluators understand the context.

Key Assumptions Made in System Design

#AssumptionRationaleImpact if Wrong
1Synthetic Data: All financial figures are illustrative and do not represent Nykaa's actual performance.Public financial data is available only at aggregate level; SBU and category-level splits are estimated.Low — the system design (schema, forms, dashboards) works regardless of data values.
2Last-Click Attribution: Default attribution model is last-click. Multi-touch attribution is available but requires configuration.Last-click is the industry standard baseline. Multi-touch requires significant data infrastructure.Medium — last-click over-attributes to bottom-funnel channels (Paid Search) and under-attributes to brand-building (Influencer, Content).
3Data Refresh Frequency: Investment data refreshes weekly; Return data refreshes daily; Store POS data refreshes EOD.Based on industry norms — ad platforms provide daily data; offline retail batches overnight.Medium — real-time capabilities would require streaming architecture (Kafka/Kinesis).
4ROPO Calculation: Online-to-Offline attribution is estimated at 6-13% based on customer matching (phone/email). The actual methodology is simplified for this prototype.True deterministic ROPO requires identity resolution across web + POS — a complex engineering problem.High — ROPO in beauty retail may be 15-25% (industry benchmarks). Underestimating ROPO undervalues online marketing's contribution to store sales.
5Attribution Windows: 30-day click-through, 1-day view-through for display; 7-day for social.Standard for e-commerce. Shorter windows for display/social due to lower intent.Low — industry-standard values unlikely to cause material misattribution.
6Fragrance Decline: The Fragrance category's negative QoQ growth is attributed to pricing competition from Tira and Amazon. This is a hypothesis — actual root cause may include supplier issues or demand shifts.Based on competitor analysis shown in Diagnostic Dashboard.Medium — if the decline is actually due to supply chain or product quality issues, the recommended marketing reallocation won't fix it.
7Marginal Yield Curves: The Scenario Planner assumes each investment category has a known return curve with diminishing marginal returns at higher spend levels. These curves are estimated from historical data patterns.This is standard in marketing mix modeling (MMM). Exact curves require econometric analysis of 2+ years of data.Medium — inaccurate curves would produce sub-optimal budget recommendations.

17 · Limitations

Why this page exists: Be honest about what the current system CANNOT do. This builds credibility and defines the roadmap for future versions.

Current Limitations

#LimitationImpactFuture Enhancement (v3.1+)
1No Live APIs: The system shows synthetic data. In production, Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, and POS APIs would feed real-time data.Data is illustrative — dashboard numbers won't match actual business performance.Integrate live API connectors for all major ad platforms and analytics tools.
2Illustrative Data Only: Revenue, margin, and investment figures are estimates based on publicly available Nykaa financial reports.Cannot be used for actual business decisions without replacing synthetic data with real data.Connect to real database; implement data validation and reconciliation with ERP.
3Simplified Attribution: Uses last-click as default; multi-touch attribution is mentioned but not fully modeled.Over-attributes value to bottom-funnel channels; under-reports brand-building ROAS.Implement multi-touch attribution (linear, time-decay, data-driven) as user-selectable options.
4Estimated ROPO: Online-to-Offline attribution uses simplified estimates (6-13%) rather than deterministic matching.May undervalue digital marketing's contribution to in-store purchases.Implement deterministic ROPO via phone/email matching across web sessions and POS transactions.
5No Predictive Analytics: The Scenario Planner uses manual what-if sliders rather than ML-based forecast models.Scenario modeling is approximate — doesn't account for interaction effects between channels.Add ML-based Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) with Bayesian inference for more accurate forecasts.
6Single Market (India): The system is designed for Nykaa's India operations only.Cannot be used for international expansion analysis (if Nykaa expands globally).Add multi-country and multi-currency support in the location hierarchy.
7No Real-time Alerts: KPIs update on daily/weekly refresh cycles — no real-time anomaly detection.Fraud, sudden traffic drops, or viral spikes are detected with delay.Implement streaming alerts with threshold-based notifications via email/Slack.
8Basic Role-Based Access: User roles are defined but not implemented with authentication in this prototype.All screens are visible to all users in the demo. In production, Store Managers should see only their store.Implement OAuth 2.0 with row-level security in the database.

18 · Project Summary

Why this page exists: A consolidated view of the entire project — business context, system design, benefits, impact, and future scope — for presentation and evaluation.

Executive Summary

Business: Nykaa — India's #1 beauty omnichannel retailer with 3 SBUs (Marketplace, Owned Brands, Offline Retail), 180+ stores, 3,000+ brands, and ₹500+ Cr annual marketing & operations spend.

Problem: Marketing and operations spend was distributed across silos (digital marketing, store operations, seller incentives) with no unified system to track which rupees drove which returns. Leadership couldn't confidently answer "where should the next ₹10 Cr go?"

Solution: A unified Marketing & Retail Decision System with:

  • 2 Fact tables (Investment & Return) as single source of truth
  • 12 Master tables for consistent dimensions
  • 6 Purpose-built dashboards for every decision-maker
  • Drill-down diagnostic from Region → Store → Campaign → Week
  • What-if Scenario Planner for budget allocation decisions
  • Standardized input forms with approval workflow

Key Insight: Offline Retail consumes 15% of investment but yields 2.6x return — well below the 3.5x threshold. Fragrance category shows negative growth despite highest margins. Scenario B (shift ₹8 Cr from Offline to Performance Marketing) projects +₹22 Cr GMV and +1.2 pts margin.

Benefits

✅ Single Source of Truth: Every number traces to a fact table row

✅ Faster Decisions: Drill-down diagnostic in <10 clicks

✅ Accountability: Every investment has an owner and approval trail

✅ ROI Clarity: Return Index for every category, channel, campaign

✅ Predictive Power: Scenario Planner shows impact before money moves

✅ Scalable Design: Add new categories, channels, stores without schema changes

Business Impact

📊 Marketing Efficiency: Identify +₹2.2 Cr in underperforming spend

💰 Margin Growth: Projected +1.2 pts from budget reallocation

🏪 Store Optimization: Flag underperformers before they bleed cash

📦 Inventory Health: 22% dead stock in Fragrance identified for clearance

🚀 Growth Trajectory: 24% CAGR supported with data-driven decisions

Future Scope

🔮 v3.1: Live API integrations (Google Ads, Meta, GA4), ML-based Marketing Mix Modeling, real-time anomaly alerts

🔮 v3.2: Deterministic ROPO matching, multi-touch attribution engine, customer-level CLV tracking

🔮 v4.0: Multi-country support, AI-powered budget auto-optimization, voice-query dashboards ("Alexa, what's Nykaa's ROAS this week?")
